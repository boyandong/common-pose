### 1. Discussion：必须让读者理解为什么会出现反向结果

A shared pose interface should be evaluated not only by whether a downstream model can accept its inputs, but also by whether the model preserves its decisions. Across six frozen consumers, learned offline ranking reduced overall disagreement, yet its class-level consequences were not uniformly beneficial.

The distinction arises because overall disagreement weights reference classes according to their frequency among retained, reference-valid blocks, whereas class-balanced disagreement gives each class equal weight. Improving one measure therefore does not guarantee improving the other. SHOW instruction illustrates this divergence within the same 79 evaluable participants, although the experiment does not establish the specific mechanism behind it. DB9 posture reveals a different limitation: after selection, only three participants retained sufficient support for evaluating every reference class. ## 八、我的最终投稿建议

从论文质量来看，我认为你现在不需要把 CommonPose 继续包装成更强的算法论文，也不需要把它延伸成未经验证的实时 HMI 系统。

它已经有一个明确且合理的 EMBC 科学贡献：

共享姿态接口的输出兼容性不保证下游决策保真；离线选择能够改善整体一致性，但整体收益可能掩盖类别恶化或支持不足。

这是一个可以独立成篇的研究问题。

我仍然认为，训练成员 provenance 和跨源物理等价的不确定性会使部分审稿人保留重大意见。一次最终写作修订不能消除这些科学限制。因此我不会保证终稿一定被接收，也不会因为它们仍然存在，就要求你无限增加实验。

我的建议是：完成上述一次性修订，经过导师的科学责任审阅和最终合规检查后，就按 EMBC 2027 的 Full Contributed Paper 正式投稿。

如果投递方向需要选择，我会优先考虑官方列出的 Biomedical Signal Processing，其次是 Wearable Biomedical Sensors and Systems；因为这篇论文的最强证据是生理信号接口的离线评估，而不是已经落地的硬件系统或临床转化成果。
