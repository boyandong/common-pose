# Data lineage and exposure

**Definitions.** `TOUCHED` means CommonPose scientific development or source inspection accessed that identity; `UNTOUCHED` means absent from known CommonPose scientific development before the final run; `PROTECTED` means a reserved split barred from development access; `UNKNOWN-UPSTREAM` means the checkpoint's actual training membership cannot be resolved. These predicates describe different histories and can coexist. After the completed final run, its 20 users are no longer untouched by *all* CommonPose work; their qualification is **untouched before final confirmation**. Official metadata `split` or `held_out_user` alone does not prove checkpoint nonexposure.
