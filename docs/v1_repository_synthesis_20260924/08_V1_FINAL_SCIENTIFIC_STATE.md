# HumanEngine / CommonPose V1: final scientific state

## Implemented, tested path

Official emg2pose paired sensing data → frozen VEMG2Pose upstream checkpoint → predicted physical joint-angle stream → frozen canonical16/trusted-8 selection and validity/alignment contract → independently DB9-trained trusted-8 eight-class LDA customer → paired substitution risk relative to the *same customer on measured GT pose* → frozen deployment-visible Gate4E or Gate4F risk score → at fixed within-user 30% acceptance, serve the lowest-score blocks and abstain on the rest. The final test used 20 users absent from prior CommonPose scientific development and 702 paired files. It is an offline scientific confirmation of customer-output preservation under selective service, not a validated streaming product.

**Established by final frozen evidence.** Requirement-U Gate4E hist-GBDT reduced ordinary customer Flip risk for 20/20 users at 30% acceptance; median paired reduction 17.08 percentage points. Requirement-B Gate4F logistic reduced class-balanced customer Flip risk for 20/20; median paired reduction 16.11 points. Both prespecified exact sign tests survived Holm adjustment (p 1.9073486e-6 each). Each final gate model and feature/threshold manifest was frozen in DEV before final outcomes. Absolute risk remains meaningful: median accepted ordinary risk 14.19% under E and median accepted balanced risk 47.48% under F. The latter is substantial, and per-class support limits remain.

**Development-stage observations.** Gate4C established heterogeneous substitution preservation on eight VALIDATION users, without a binary pass criterion. Gate4D Gate A ranked continuous Fisher `e_F` on 19 DEV users, 18/19 improved at 30%; this is a different endpoint. Gate4E and F selection and relative candidate ranking are DEV results. The final lane confirmed their *frozen primary customer contracts*, not every DEV mechanism, score superiority, or threshold-coverage guarantee.

**Unresolved.** The checkpoint's actual training-user membership, cross-source independent physical zero/calibration equivalence, population representativeness, broader sensor-backbone/API interoperability, semantic gesture truth, online serving latency and deployment threshold coverage guarantees cannot be established from the frozen final artifacts. The final cohort is **CommonPose scientific-development untouched before confirmation**, not proven **upstream-unseen**. The final fixed within-user 30% rank rule is different from holding out a single calibrated global threshold at exactly 30% for every future user.

**Do not claim.** Do not call Flip human gesture error, hard agreement semantic accuracy, 83.42% accepted E ACC full-system accuracy, 20 users upstream-unseen, Gate4B an outcome experiment, or either E/F a universal winner. Gate4E and Gate4F encode different customer contracts: ordinary usage-weighted Flip versus class-balanced Flip. Their secondary losses show why choosing one “champion” would erase the requirement. The V1 contribution is a bounded, falsifiable, customer-specific selective-reliability result on a frozen combined path.
