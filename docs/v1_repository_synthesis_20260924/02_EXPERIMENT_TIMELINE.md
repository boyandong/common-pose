# Scientific timeline

Sequence is scientific dependency, not a claim that every earlier artifact has a reliable creation timestamp. Most final gate decisions are dated 2026-09-24; historical source dates are recorded in their own reports.

1. Dataset admission and DB9 source/channel forensics established that coordinate labels and sensor channels needed physical audit. A 37-person interim verdict was extended to all 77; the frozen trusted eight channels remained stable. KIN-MUS `shared10` anatomical interpretation was later withdrawn.
2. DB9 canonical16 posture work established a six-class historical head and 34-development/43-outcome holdout (`docs/CONFIRMATORY_RESULTS.md`). Later trusted-8, eight-class LOSO utility was 0.7479 BA across 77. These are distinct tasks, class lists, and protocols.
3. Single-user bridge and KIN-MUS observability diagnostics tested narrower questions. The bridge's ~76.5% agreement involved a different six-class pipeline; it is not Gate3's 36.45% or Gate4C's 65.71%. KIN-MUS phase needed local temporal state and its anatomical `shared10` interpretation was invalidated.
4. Pre-substitution reconciliation found all eight anatomical correspondences but cross-source numerical calibration unresolved. Gate1.5 found no independent local zero anchor. Gate2A found structured GT-only customer output, not semantic correctness.
5. Gate3 ran paired measured GT pose versus frozen upstream prediction on one mini-corpus user: 86,833 50-Hz eligible blocks, 0.3645 hard agreement and 0.2868 balanced preservation. Gate3B located partial seven-dimensional Fisher representation preservation. Gate4A screened deployment-visible ranking on that one user; it selected no formal gate.
6. Gate4B froze 20 DEV/8 VALIDATION sessions (964 files) for multi-user reliability but closed at source-authentic structural failure before any scientific outcome. No Gate4B effect may be cited.
7. Gate4C prospectively excluded the structurally failed session and froze a distinct 19 DEV/8 VALIDATION, 926-file substitution study. Eight VALIDATION users gave median 0.6571 hard preservation and 0.3331 balanced preservation. The heterogeneous result closed descriptively.
8. Gate4D reused 19 DEV users/654 files for continuous Fisher task-space reliability development. Gate A was strongest for its registered continuous objective. This did not establish a final customer Flip gate.
9. Gate4E froze Requirement-U, ordinary usage-weighted customer Flip, selecting hist-GBDT on DEV. Gate4F froze Requirement-B, class-balanced Flip, selecting logistic on the same DEV cohort. Both are development selections.
10. Metadata-only cohort audit counted 164 CommonPose-untouched eligible identities but could not prove upstream training nonexposure. A later authorized final plan froze 20 users and 702 files, then completed joint untouched confirmation for both gates. Both prespecified paired user directions were positive in 20/20 users; the claim remains CommonPose-development untouched, upstream exposure unknown.
